## Final prompt

```text
Use case: ads-marketing. Create one photorealistic high-end BeautyPass skincare campaign photograph, portrait 2:3.
References: uploaded KS1 and AS4 guide the luminous hydrated skin finish with visible pores; KS3 guides the sculptural light and graceful head angle; AS5 guides editorial polish, not its crystals. These are aesthetic references, not identities to copy.
Concept: a quiet getting-ready moment by a large sunlit window. A beautiful clearly adult Korean woman age 25–28, dark almond-shaped eyes, natural straight brows, black hair with a precise center part smoothed into a low bun. She turns her upper body away and looks calmly back toward the camera in a gentle three-quarter angle, chin level. Her near cheek catches the light. One elegant hand lightly adjusts a single small sculptural gold stud at her ear, staying beside the ear and never covering or touching the cheek. Natural anatomically correct hand, understated short nude manicure. She wears a modest chocolate-brown silk camisole with a softly draped matching silk wrap low around her shoulders; fabric stays below the collarbones. One tiny gold earring, no necklace.
The emotional subject is her effortless composure, but the visual subject is the skin. Fresh glass-skin luminosity: broad creamy specular highlight across the near cheekbone, smaller highlights on nose bridge and brow bone, luminous collarbone, subtle rose-beige lip gloss, softly flushed cheeks. Real fine pores and realistic skin texture. Dewy, hydrated and youthful adult, not oily, sweaty, plastic or heavily airbrushed. Minimal makeup, no strong eyeliner or glam eyelashes.
Directional late-afternoon window light from camera left, soft warm shadow on the far cheek with detail retained, a warm ivory/taupe plaster wall behind her. A very soft unfocused strip of sheer curtain at the far edge suggests the window without adding clutter. The light should be clearer and fresher than a dark perfume ad, but richer and more dimensional than flat clinical skincare stock photography. Warm ivory, soft honey skin highlights, espresso hair, cocoa silk. Do not tint everything orange.
Composition: editorial medium close portrait from above the whole head to mid chest. The head is fully included with 6 percent breathing room at top, face positioned in upper 55 percent of image, eyes around 30 percent image height. Both eyes, forehead and highlighted cheek visible, graceful natural neck, the hand beside ear in middle-right. Face must remain readable when the sides are cropped to a narrow mobile frame. The lower 24 percent is mainly quiet chocolate silk in soft folds for website white text that will be added separately. Focus sharp on skin and eyes, fabric and background softer. 85mm portrait photography, premium fashion magazine campaign, subtle film grain. Original believable model, not a celebrity.
Avoid spa bed, towels, headband, practitioners, face massage, skincare bottles, tools, crystals, sunglasses, coffee, heavy jewelry, excessive wet shine, childish facial proportions, overly sculpted doll face, extra fingers, text, logos, borders.
```
