Use case: photorealistic-natural.

Create a NEW visual from scratch, not the previous casual woman wearing knitwear. The image must feel sharply art-directed, sleek, sculptural, and sensorial, with glossy skin immediately the clear subject.

Long graceful but anatomically normal neck, clean jawline, natural facial proportions. Her hair is intensely neat, glossy jet-black, perfectly combed close to the scalp with a precise center part, pulled into a straight low ponytail entirely behind her back and not visible around the neck. No bun, wisps, baby-hair styling, flyaways, curls, or loose strands.

Pose and composition: an upright sculptural bust portrait. Body angled slightly away from the lens, shoulders form an asymmetric diagonal, head turned gently about 25 degrees toward camera-left and chin raised just a little toward the light. Bare shoulders and neckline; tasteful crop above the bust, no visible breasts and no visible garment, straps, towel or fabric. No jewelry.
